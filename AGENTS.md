# Repository Guidance

## Public content

Before creating or editing any indexable landing page, blog article, gallery description, metadata, structured data, or sitemap entry, read and follow `CONTENT_STANDARD.md`.

- Keep one primary search intent per page and update the keyword map when a new page is added.
- Do not publish a new article without metadata, visible authorship and dates, internal links, matching structured data, a blog card, and a sitemap entry.
- Keep Persian copy natural and human-edited. Do not use keyword stuffing, fabricated claims, hidden SEO text, or misleading product capabilities.
- Run HTML and JSON-LD validation before deployment.

## Article UI

Before creating or editing a blog article or the blog index, read and follow `ARTICLE_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` in addition to `CONTENT_STANDARD.md`.

- Reuse the required article DOM skeleton and shared class names instead of inventing a new article layout.
- Keep the table of contents on the right on desktop RTL layouts and the article title centered on desktop.
- Validate the article at the desktop and mobile viewports defined in the visual standard before deployment.
- Do not publish a visually divergent article unless the design standard is intentionally updated at the same time.
